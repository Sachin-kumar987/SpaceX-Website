Space Launches Timeline

JUL 12, 2025 – CRS-25

SpaceX launched a Cargo Resupply Service mission to the ISS using Falcon 9 and Dragon.

JUN 30, 2025 – Starlink Group 8

Another batch of 52 Starlink satellites was successfully deployed to expand global internet coverage.

JUN 20, 2025 – SES-22 Mission

Launch of a communications satellite supporting digital TV services across North America.

JUN 10, 2025 – Globalstar FM15

This mission launched a satellite to enhance voice and data services in remote areas.

MAY 18, 2025 – Transporter-8

A rideshare mission with over 90 small satellites launched into sun-synchronous orbit.
